Installing NetBird
  1. Get an invite from ghazi first. Without it, sign-in won't work.
  2. Get NetBird P2P VPN from the App Store.
  3. Open it and tap connect.
  4. Tap Allow when it asks to add a VPN.
  5. Sign in with the email and password from the invite.
  6. It should say Connected.
