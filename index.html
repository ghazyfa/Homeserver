Install NetBird

Set up your device

Do this once on each phone, tablet or computer you want to use. We picked your device from your browser; change it if that's wrong.

  1. Request access from ghazi first with the invite form. Without an invite from ghazi, NetBird sign-in won't work.
  2. Install NetBird P2P VPN from the App Store.
  3. Open NetBird and tap the big connect button.
  4. When iPhone asks to add VPN configurations, tap Allow. You may need Face ID or your passcode.
  5. A sign-in page opens. Sign in with the email and password from your NetBird invitation.
  6. Go back to the NetBird app. It should say Connected.

You can also switch it on and off in iPhone Settings > VPN.

Questions

Before you start

Why can't I open the server without NetBird?
Nothing is open to the internet. It works on the home Wi-Fi, or anywhere else with NetBird on. Then tvserver.homes opens Home instead of this page.
Can I leave NetBird on all the time?
Yes. It only carries server traffic, so the rest of your internet works as usual.
I turned NetBird on and still see this page.
Give it a minute, then reload. Your device can remember the old address for a short while.
